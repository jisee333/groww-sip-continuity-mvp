5. Product rationale & measurement

Where AI earns its place

A conversational explanation can interpret a user's natural-language question against several return periods and the current portfolio snapshot. A static table cannot adapt its explanation to the question or clearly handle uncertainty.

What non-AI still does better

Calculating and displaying verified return percentages should remain deterministic. AI is only the explanation layer; it must never calculate source returns or recommend a fund.

Experiment hypothesis: showing verified 1Y/3Y/5Y return context with a grounded explanation may improve users' understanding of performance. Validate comprehension and 12-month active-SIP retention in a controlled experiment; do not assume retention impact.

Suggested measures: return-context view rate · correct understanding in a comprehension test · explanation quality-gate pass rate · AI fallback rate · 12-month active SIP rate (north star: at least one successful instalment in the last 30 days). Guardrails: complaints, mistaken interpretation, cancellations and support contacts.
